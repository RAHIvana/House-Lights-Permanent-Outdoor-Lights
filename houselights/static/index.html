    Settings

    Change ZIP / time zone Change setup

    Controllers, runs of lights, time zone and location are set in the setup wizard. You can jump to any step of the wizard by tapping it in the list at its top, and test one run with its Test button below. Changing them restarts the server; schedules and themes are kept.

    Each light run's strip is drawn the way it's installed: light 1 is at the end you set in the setup wizard (left, or right for a run that starts at its right end), so animations move across the screen the way they move on the house. One segment = 5 lights. Apply sends a static theme as segment colors, and they stay on the kit. Play live streams every light, which animated themes need. It runs until you press Stop or switch the kit off.
