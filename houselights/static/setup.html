What lights do you have?

House Lights talks to the controllers over your home network. Tick every kind you have; you can mix them.

Find your controllers

Scanning only asks controllers who they are; it changes nothing.

    If your lights are on a different network or subnet than this computer, type that network (like 192.168.2.0/24) or the controllers' addresses. Tuya devices are only heard on this computer's own network.

    Name and count each run

    A run is the string of lights on one controller (or one JellyFish zone).

    If you don't know how many lights you have, leave it blank: the Test step helps you count them.

    Some runs may be installed left to right and others right to left. "Light 1 is at" lets every run still show the right way round on the dashboard.

    Put the runs in order along the house, left to right as you face it, with ▲ and ▼. Themes that play across the whole house use this order.
