What lights do you have?

House Lights talks to the controllers over your home network. Tick every kind you have; you can mix them.

Find your controllers

Scanning only asks controllers who they are; it changes nothing.

    If your lights are on a different network or subnet than this computer, type that network (like 192.168.2.0/24) or the controllers' addresses. Tuya devices are only heard on this computer's own network.
